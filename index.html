Hi, I'm Faiaz Ben Reza, Associate Business Analyst and Product Owner at Brain Station 23, driving Mobile Sales Force Automation (MSFA) solutions for leading pharmaceutical companies in Bangladesh.

I'm a versatile Business Analyst and Project Management professional with expertise in requirement gathering, BRDs, user stories, process flows, and wireframes. Experienced in Agile/Scrum project planning, sprint management, and stakeholder coordination to ensure timely delivery and measurable business outcomes.

Currently managing MSFA product delivery for Sharif Pharmaceutical, Beacon Pharmaceutical, and Popular Pharmaceutical, bridging the gap between business needs and technical execution through structured documentation, sprint ceremonies, and cross-functional collaboration.

I hold a BSc in Computer Science & Engineering from AIUB and previously served as Assistant General Secretary of the AIUB Computer Club, organizing flagship events like Blockchain Olympiad Bangladesh 2022 and ICCA 2022, earning a university scholarship for extraordinary club contributions.

Equally comfortable leading sprint ceremonies, facilitating AI workshops, or producing business development content, I bring a blend of analytical rigour, communication clarity, and a continuous-improvement mindset to every project.
