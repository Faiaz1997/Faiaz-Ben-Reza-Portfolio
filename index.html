02 · A bit about me

The person beyond
the job title.

I’m Faiaz Ben Reza, a Business Analyst at Brain Station 23 and the Product Owner of SalesPro23. My work covers market research, feature decisions, demo prototypes, development coordination, and planning how we take the product to market.

I started with a BSc in Computer Science & Engineering and hands-on development, which helps me read API specifications, discuss architecture, and work through requirements with developers. I also use ChatGPT, Claude AI, and Gemini, with prompt engineering, to turn research and notes into BRDs, user stories, and material for backlog discussions.

Alongside SalesPro23, I’ve worked on MSFA delivery for Sharif, Beacon, and Popular Pharmaceutical. Outside work, I served as Assistant General Secretary of the AIUB Computer Club and helped organize Blockchain Olympiad Bangladesh 2022 and ICCA 2022.
