I'm Faiaz Ben Reza, a Business Analyst I at Brain Station 23 and the Product Owner of SalesPro23. My work covers market research, feature decisions, demo prototypes, development coordination, and planning how we take the product to market.

I started with a BSc in Computer Science & Engineering and hands-on development experience. That background helps me read API specifications, discuss system architecture, and work through requirements with developers.

I use ChatGPT, Claude AI, and Gemini for requirement analysis, BRDs, user stories, and market research. Prompt engineering helps me turn research and notes into structured material for backlog discussions and stakeholder presentations.

I've also worked on MSFA delivery for Sharif, Beacon, and Popular Pharmaceutical, including documentation, sprint ceremonies, and coordination across teams. Our MSFA product has since evolved into SalesPro23, a SaaS platform for FMCG and pharma businesses.

Outside my day job, I served as Assistant General Secretary of the AIUB Computer Club. I helped organize Blockchain Olympiad Bangladesh 2022 and ICCA 2022, and received a university scholarship for my club contributions.
